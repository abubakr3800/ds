2. Body and Heat-Sink
2.1 Heat-Sink Operation Theory

Due to electronic circuits nature, there is over internal heat generated during the operation period. That heat needs to be dissipated to protect driver components from being damaged.

According to Heat Transfer Theory, materials with high thermal conductivity are used to transfer heat from the hot region to the cold one, space in our case, so a heat-sink made from Aluminum alloy is a suitable choice for this mission.
